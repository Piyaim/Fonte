Fonte is a weight-training log that runs entirely on your phone: no server, no account, nothing sent anywhere.

Features: editable catalogue of 50 exercises, programs and sessions, sessions by muscle group, perceived-effort gauge with load advice, a rest timer whose display stays accurate when the screen locks, personal records, progress charts, body weight, progress photos, import and export.

Your data stays in the app: export it regularly, it is erased if you uninstall the app. The interface is in French.

Fonte is not a coach and gives no medical advice.
